What They Don't Tell You About Making a Robot Arm Grab Things

Stefano Maestri / PyCon 2026

Abstract

A practical journey through robot learning — from hardware nightmares to working policies, and how cloud platforms are making it accessible to every developer. This talk covers the full stack of getting a SO-101 robot arm to reliably pick up objects: assembly pitfalls, camera calibration, policy training on consumer GPUs, the diagnostic tools that actually made it work, and what the cloud alternative looks like today.
